:::info
The URL is selected by an administrator and as a user you will receive an **email invitation** from your admin to join.
:::

The URL you will ultimately use to manage your credentials may vary depending on whether your Haspr instance is
self-hosted or used in the cloud.

- If you're accessing Haspr through a self-hosted setup, the URL will likely reflect the specific domain of your organisation,
for example `haspr.your-organisation.com` or `your-organisation.com/haspr`.
- Conversely, if you're utilizing Haspr through a cloud service, the URL will typically be `cloud.haspr.dev/your-organisation`.

Long story short, it depends on your situation, as Haspr can be hosted on-premises or in the cloud.

## How can I find the login page?

### If you have completed the setup

If you have completed the setup and configured Haspr on your current laptop or desktop, you can click on the Haspr icon in the top right corner of your browser.
If you then click on the Haspr logo it will take you to your Haspr workspace.

### Check for Haspr emails in your mailbox

In most cases you will have received an email notification from Haspr in the past in your mailbox.
So check your inbox and follow one of the links.

### Go on [haspr.dev](https://haspr.dev)

If you have completed your setup and configured Haspr on your current computer but you don't find the Haspr icon on your browser, you can go to [haspr.dev](https://haspr.dev).
On the top of the website you will see a "Sign in" button you can click on.
This button will automatically redirect you to where your browser extension has been configured and you will see the login form of your application.

### Ask for help from your administrator

When in doubt you can also ask the person that invited you to Haspr, e.g. the administrator
that set up Haspr for your company.

## Other clues

### You are using Haspr cloud version

If you are using Haspr cloud your passwords will be located in a workspace in https://cloud.haspr.dev/workspace, where workspace is the name of your organisation, like https://cloud.haspr.dev/acme.

### You are using Haspr self-hosted version

If you are using the self-hosted version of Haspr you can contact your administrator, as the self-hosted version, much like a blog, can be hosted anywhere.
